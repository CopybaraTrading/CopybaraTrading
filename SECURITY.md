# Security

## Reporting a problem

If you find a security problem in Copybara, email **support.copybara@gmail.com** with "Security" in the subject.

- **Please don't** open a public issue, post it on X or Telegram, or test it against other people's accounts or funds.
- **Include** what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it.
- **We will** reply within 3 days, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you when it is fixed, if you'd like.

If you think funds are at risk right now, say so in the subject line. We can stop all trading at once while we look.

## What counts

Anything that could let someone:

- move, trade or withdraw funds from a wallet that isn't theirs;
- get Copybara to sign something outside the rules in [docs/SECURITY_MODEL.md](docs/SECURITY_MODEL.md);
- sign in as someone else, or read another user's account;
- change another user's copies or settings.

Bugs that don't touch security, such as a page that looks wrong or a trade that was skipped, are welcome as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/CopybaraTrading/CopybaraTrading/issues/new/choose).

## Scams

Copybara will never ask for your private keys, seed phrase, or a payment to "unlock" or "verify" your wallet. Only trust links and contract addresses published on [copybara.trading](https://copybara.trading).

# Copybara 🦫

**Calm bot. Fast copy.** Paste any wallet and Copybara makes the same trades for you, sized and sold by your own rules.

🌐 [copybara.trading](https://copybara.trading) · 𝕏 [@CopybaraTrades](https://x.com/CopybaraTrades) · ✈️ [Telegram](https://t.me/CopybaraTrades)

## What it does

- **Copy any wallet** on Robinhood Chain, Solana and Elysium, with more chains coming soon.
- **Your size, your exits.** Set how much goes into each copy, and take profit, stop loss or sell when they sell.
- **Fast.** Copybara watches the wallets you pick as their trades land, so your copy follows within seconds.

## Built so nobody else can touch your funds

- **Your keys stay with Privy.** Your trading wallet is held by Privy, which is independently audited. Copybara never sees your keys, and you can export them at any time.
- **The bot can only trade.** It can only trade through approved exchanges and move your funds between your own wallets, apart from Copybara's fees. Every trade is checked by two separate systems.
- **Stop whenever you like.** Pause a copy, turn the bot off, or withdraw back to your wallet. One switch stops everything.

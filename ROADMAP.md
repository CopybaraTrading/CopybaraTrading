# Roadmap

The live roadmap is at [copybara.trading/roadmap](https://copybara.trading/roadmap). In short:

## Live now

- Private beta: copy trading on Robinhood Chain, Solana and Elysium.
- Polymarket: top bettors ranked by win rate and profit, and copying their bets.

## Upcoming

- Open to everyone: no beta code needed.
- Base, BNB Chain and Ethereum switched back on, then Avalanche.
- Inverse trading: do the opposite of a wallet's trades.

## Later

Shaped by what testers ask for. Tell us on [Telegram](https://t.me/CopybaraTrades) or [share an idea](https://github.com/CopybaraTrading/CopybaraTrading/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
